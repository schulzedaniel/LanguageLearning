## Swedish for English speakers: what to prioritise
- **Pronunciation early, little and often:** the vowels **å, ä, ö** and **u/y**, long vs. short vowels (**tak** vs. **tack**, **glas** vs. **glass**), the sj-sound (**sju, sked, skjorta, station**) and tj-sound (**tjugo, kjol, kök**), **k/g/sk** softening before e, i, y, ä, ö (**kyrka, göra, skina**), and that **o** is often pronounced like "oo". Mention the pitch accent (**anden / anden**) only lightly at first; praise good melody.
- **Grammar, in this order of importance:** verbs don't change with the person (**jag är, du är, vi är**), present tense ending **-r**; the **V2 rule** (the verb is the second element in a main clause: **Idag dricker jag kaffe**, never "Idag jag dricker"); **en/ett** nouns and the definite suffix (**en bil → bilen, ett hus → huset**); the position of **inte** (after the verb in main clauses, before the verb in subordinate clauses: **…att jag inte kan**); adjective agreement (**en stor bil, ett stort hus, stora bilar**) and later double definiteness (**den stora bilen**); past tense and perfect (**åt / har ätit**).
- **Typical English-speaker errors to listen for:** putting the subject before the verb after a fronted adverb ("Idag jag…"), forgetting the -r of the present, "jag är 30 år" (correct!) vs. "jag har 30 år" (that's French thinking), using **ha** instead of **har**, mixing up **på / i / till**, and false friends: **eventuellt** = possibly, **semester** = holiday, **rolig** = fun, **glass** = ice cream, **kissa** has nothing to do with kissing, **bra** = good.
- **Use the gift of shared vocabulary:** many words are close to English (**hus, bok, finger, vinter, hand, båt**). Point this out to beginners: it builds confidence fast.
- **Culture adds motivation:** fika, lagom, allemansrätten, midsommar, Lucia, kräftskiva, Swedish directness and love of nature, the importance of being on time, "jantelagen". Weave in one short cultural note when it fits the topic, never as a lecture.
- **Swedex and TISUS habits from the start:** full-sentence answers, asking questions back, describing your everyday life, and later giving reasons with **eftersom / för att** and opinions with **jag tycker att…**.
